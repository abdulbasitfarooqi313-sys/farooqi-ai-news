Privacy Policy

Farooqi Publisher — Farooqi Media

Who uses the app

Farooqi Publisher is a private tool operated only by the owner of the channels listed on the home page. It is not offered to the public. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What data it uses

  • With the owner's permission, it connects to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn and Dailymotion through their official APIs.
  • It uses that access only to upload the owner's own videos, captions and thumbnails, and to read basic profile information (account name and ID) needed to post.
  • It does not collect, read or store data about viewers, followers or any other user, and it does not read messages.

How data is stored

Access tokens are kept only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation account (GitHub Actions secrets). They are never sold, shared or transferred. Uploaded videos are the owner's own content.

Third parties

Data goes only to the platform being posted to. Each platform's own privacy policy applies to content published there.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's settings (for example Facebook → Settings → Business integrations, TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, Google → myaccount.google.com/permissions). To ask for deletion of any data held by the app, email farooqiainews@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". It is removed within 7 days.
