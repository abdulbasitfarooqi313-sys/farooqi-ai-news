📱 Short 1: ChatGPT Saved Your Files?

Hook: Deleting your ChatGPT chat does not delete your uploaded files.

When you upload a document or image to ChatGPT, it gets saved to your account Library. Deleting the chat conversation leaves the file sitting in your cloud storage limit. To clear it, open your profile settings, click My Library, select your uploaded files, and check the permanent delete box.
