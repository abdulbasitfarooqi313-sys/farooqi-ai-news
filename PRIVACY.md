# Privacy Policy — Farooqi AI News Uploader

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

**Farooqi AI News Uploader** is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel
**Farooqi AI News (فاروقی اے آئی نیوز)** to upload and schedule the channel's own videos.

## What data the app uses
- The app uses the **YouTube Data API** with the owner's permission (scopes `youtube.upload` and `youtube`)
  only to upload videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and schedules **to the owner's own channel**.
- It does **not** read, collect or store any data about viewers or any other YouTube user.
- The access token is stored only as an encrypted **GitHub Actions Secret** of this repository and is never
  shared, sold or transferred to anyone.

## Third parties
- No data is shared with third parties. The app talks only to Google/YouTube APIs.
- Use of YouTube is governed by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Revoking access
The owner can remove the app's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
