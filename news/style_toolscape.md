# Toolscape style guide (read on every run)

## Voice
- Friendly, simple English. Short sentences. Talk to "you". No hype words ("insane", "game-changer", "mind-blowing").
- Honest: always one real downside. Never invent prices, dates or numbers.

## Hook (first 15 seconds)
- Open with the RESULT, not the introduction: "In the next 7 minutes you will turn a messy list into a finished report."
- One of these patterns: clear result + time ("...in 5 minutes"), common pain ("Still doing X by hand?"),
  surprising free option ("This free tool does what a $20 app does"), mistake to avoid.
- Never start with "Hi guys", "Welcome back" or the channel name.

## Titles
- Under 65 characters. Benefit first, tool name second. One number if natural.
- Must match exactly what the video shows. No fake urgency.

## Every scene
- Show on screen exactly what is being said. Concrete clicks, exact prompts, real values.
- One idea per scene. Beginner should be able to pause and copy.

## Ending
- Quick recap in one sentence, subscribe call, tease the next related video.
